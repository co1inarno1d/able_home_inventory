Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 30, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 30, 2026

Able Home Accessibility ("we," "our," or "us") operates an internal inventory and operations management application (the "App") used exclusively by our staff to manage stairlift and ramp equipment, scheduling, and customer records.

1. Who This Policy Covers

This App is a private, internal business tool. It is not available to the general public. Access is limited to authorized Able Home Accessibility employees and contractors.

2. Information We Access via QuickBooks

The App connects to QuickBooks Online via the Intuit API to allow staff to search and view customer records stored in our QuickBooks account. Specifically, the App:

  • Reads customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, and billing addresses from QuickBooks for the purpose of linking equipment records to customers.
  • Does not write to, modify, or delete any QuickBooks data.
  • Does not access financial data, invoices, payments, or accounting information.
  • Does not share QuickBooks data with any third party.

3. OAuth Tokens

When our staff connects the App to QuickBooks, an OAuth 2.0 access token and refresh token are stored in our Supabase database (hosted by Supabase Inc. in the United States). These tokens are used solely to make API requests on behalf of our own QuickBooks account. They are never shared with third parties.

4. Data We Store Internally

The App stores the following data in our private Supabase database:

  • Equipment records (stairlifts and ramps): serial numbers, status, location, condition, install dates.
  • Customer records: name, address, phone, email, funding source, and QuickBooks customer ID.
  • Schedule and operations job records.
  • Staff profiles: name, email, and role within the App.
  • Audit log entries recording which staff member made changes and when.

This data is used solely to operate our business and is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing or any other purpose.

5. Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as it is needed to operate our business. Staff may delete records at any time. OAuth tokens are deleted when the QuickBooks connection is disconnected through the App.

6. Security

Access to the App requires individual login credentials. All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Our database is protected by row-level security policies that restrict access to authenticated staff only.

7. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database and storage hosting (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Intuit / QuickBooks Online — accounting and customer data (intuit.com/privacy)
  • Netlify — web application hosting (netlify.com/privacy; app served at inventory.ableha.com)

8. Your Rights

If you are a customer of Able Home Accessibility and have questions about what personal information we hold, please contact us at the address below. We do not sell personal information and will respond to any access or deletion requests within 30 days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our App evolves. The effective date above will be updated with any material changes.
